Thesis & Research

A Hybrid Deep Feature and Machine-Learning Ensemble Framework for Explainable Multi-Leaf Disease Detection with Mobile Deployment

This final-year research developed a hybrid framework combining deep-feature extraction, PCA-based dimensionality reduction and machine-learning classifiers.

  • Applied image preprocessing and Random Oversampling to improve dataset quality and class balance.
  • Implemented Random Forest, LightGBM, CatBoost, Voting and Stacking Ensemble classifiers.
  • Integrated Grad-CAM for explainable AI and proposed a Flutter-based mobile application.
  • Reported 94% accuracy using Voting and Stacking Ensemble models on 311 test samples.

The mobile application was proposed as part of the project scope. Research details are based on the information supplied in the CV.
